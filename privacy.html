Privacy policy

Calendar Stom (M-Stom's Google Calendar connection) · effective 17 November 2026

What it accesses

With the clinic's permission, Calendar Stom reads and writes events in the one Google Calendar that the clinic connects (scope calendar.events). It reads events from 30 days back to 180 days ahead, so that appointments added on the phone appear in M-Stom and busy times are not double-booked. It creates and updates only the events for the clinic's own appointments.

Where the data stays

Everything Calendar Stom receives from Google stays on the clinic's own computer, inside M-Stom's encrypted records and backups. The Google sign-in is stored on that computer only, protected by Windows. There is no Calendar Stom server: nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Sharing

Data from Google is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train any model. It is used only to show and keep the clinic's appointments in step between M-Stom and Google Calendar.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar Stom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The clinic can disconnect at any time in M-Stom (Settings → Calendar & messages → Disconnect), which removes the saved sign-in from the computer, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Appointment data already in M-Stom stays in the clinic's records until the clinic deletes it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Bl4ckV4p0r/calendar-stom.
