Calendar Stom

The Google Calendar connection of M-Stom, a dental clinic's desktop application.

M-Stom is a Windows application that one dental clinic uses to keep its own patient records and appointments on its own computer. Calendar Stom is the part that connects it to the clinic's own Google Calendar:

  • appointments booked in M-Stom appear in the clinic's Google Calendar, so the dentist sees them on his phone;
  • appointments the dentist adds in Google Calendar on his phone appear in M-Stom.

It is used only by the clinic that runs it, with its own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no server: the application talks to Google Calendar directly from the clinic's computer.
